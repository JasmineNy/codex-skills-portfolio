# Four-part Application Method

## Evidence level

- Source: [赵清衡-AI产品求职陪跑｜求职焚诀：BOSS上已读不回？](https://www.douyin.com/video/7677893528252443126)
- Method: `背景介绍 + 简历 + AI作品集 + 专业图`.
- The creator's reported response improvement is an experience claim, not an independently verified result or guarantee.

## Current text-first behavior

1. Present a three-paragraph background message in the conversation.
2. Recommend one primary and one backup registered resume with direct-readiness labels and clickable paths.
3. State that the AI portfolio is unregistered; do not include recruiter-facing placeholder copy.
4. State that professional images are unregistered; do not infer or create them.

Do not create a per-job folder. Do not render resume images unless the user explicitly asks.

## Copy rules

- Greeting and one-shot: three short paragraphs, `80–140` Chinese characters total.
- Follow-up: one or two short paragraphs, `30–80` Chinese characters total.
- Paragraph 1: role interest and positioning.
- Paragraph 2: two relevant capabilities.
- Paragraph 3: one conservative result or project and a low-pressure call to action.
- Keep the tone conversational. Avoid dense prose, keyword walls, flattery, and cover-letter language.
- Never write `已附上`, `请查收`, or another attachment claim unless the user explicitly says the attachment has already been sent.
- Do not repeat phone or email in the greeting.
